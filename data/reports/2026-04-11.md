# Weekly Briefing — April 11, 2026

## Top Stories

**Anthropic's Claude Mythos: Too Dangerous to Release**  
Anthropic announced Claude Mythos Preview, a frontier model with unprecedented cybersecurity capabilities that the company has chosen *not* to release publicly. The model autonomously discovered thousands of high-severity vulnerabilities across every major operating system and web browser, and in one demonstration, developed a multi-step exploit to break out of its sandbox and email a researcher. Access is restricted to approximately 40-50 partners through Project Glasswing, a coordinated defensive initiative including AWS, Apple, Microsoft, Cisco, and CrowdStrike. Anthropic's decision marks the first time since GPT-2 in 2019 that a major lab has withheld a model due to safety concerns, reigniting debate about open-weight AI and the balance between transparency and security. [Anthropic Red Team](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/mythos-preview/), [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/why-anthropic-sent-its-claude-ai-to-an-actual-psychiatrist/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/04/anthropics-claude-mythos-finds.html), [Understanding AI](https://www.understandingai.org/p/why-anthropic-believes-its-latest)

**AI Compute Crunch Intensifies Across Industry**  
Multiple signals point to severe capacity constraints hitting AI providers. AWS reportedly lost a $10 million Fortnite hosting contract because it couldn't guarantee compute availability. OpenAI's API traffic jumped 2.5x in five months (6B to 15B tokens/minute), while Anthropic tightened usage limits affecting 7% of users and their pricing per token falls faster than revenue rises. Google's TPUs are running at full utilization across all seven generations, and H100 rental prices hit an 18-month high. The squeeze is forcing providers to ration access and pass on opportunities—OpenAI's CFO publicly stated they're declining work due to insufficient compute. [Exponential View](https://www.exponentialview.co/p/data-to-start-your-week-the-ai-squeeze), [Exponential View](https://www.exponentialview.co/p/ev-568)

**AWS Launches S3 Files: Object Storage Meets File Systems**  
AWS announced Amazon S3 Files, making S3 buckets accessible as NFS file systems with automatic synchronization. The service allows multiple compute resources (EC2, ECS, EKS, Lambda) to mount S3 storage as a traditional file system, enabling data sharing across clusters without duplication. AWS claims this makes S3 "the first and only cloud object store offering fully-featured, high-performance file system access." The launch addresses a long-standing friction point for workloads that need both object storage economics and file system semantics. [AWS News Blog](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/launching-s3-files-making-s3-buckets-accessible-as-file-systems/)

**Supply Chain Attacks Hit Developer Tools**  
Multiple developer tool compromises emerged this week. CPUID's website was hacked to serve malware through CPU-Z and HWMonitor downloads. Smart Slider 3 Pro's update system was hijacked to distribute backdoored versions to WordPress and Joomla users (800,000+ active installations). North Korean actors published 1,700 malicious packages across npm, PyPI, Go, and Rust in the "Contagious Interview" campaign. **Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration Accelerates**  
Cloudflare announced plans to achieve full post-quantum security by 2029, including authentication. The timeline was accelerated after Google announced a breakthrough quantum algorithm and Oratomic published research showing only 10,000 qubits are needed to break P-256 elliptic curve cryptography—far fewer than previously estimated. Over 65% of human traffic to Cloudflare already uses post-quantum encryption. Google separately announced its own 2029 transition target, framing the move as valuable for crypto-agility regardless of quantum timeline. [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/post-quantum-roadmap/), [Schneier on Security](https://www.schneier.com/blog/archives/2026/04/google-wants-to-transition-to-post-quantum-cryptography-by-2029.html)

**Google Launches Gemma 4 for Edge AI Agents**  
Google DeepMind released Gemma 4, a family of open models designed for multi-step planning and autonomous agentic workflows on-device. The models run on mobile devices, desktops, and IoT platforms including Raspberry Pi, support over 140 languages, and are available under Apache 2.0 license. The release includes the Google AI Edge Gallery iPhone app and LiteRT-LM library. Gemma 4 crossed 2 million downloads in its first week—faster adoption than Gemma 3's 6.7M downloads over a full year. Multiple users reported running the models on consumer Apple hardware at roughly 40 tokens/second. [Google AI](https://developers.googleblog.com/bring-state-of-the-art-agentic-skills-to-the-edge-with-gemma-4/), [Latent Space](https://www.latent.space/p/ainews-gemma-4-crosses-2-million)
